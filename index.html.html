01 — The trail you leave

Your digital footprint is bigger than you think.

A digital footprint is the trail of information created by your online activity. Some traces are shared intentionally; others are generated as you use digital services.

⌕
ACTIVE FOOTPRINT

What you choose to share

Posts, comments, photos, reviews and profile details you deliberately publish online.

◉
PASSIVE FOOTPRINT

What gets generated

Browsing activity, device information, approximate location and usage patterns collected by services.

⌁
CONNECTED DATA

Small clues add up

Separate pieces of information can be combined to infer interests, routines or preferences.

05 — Take back control

Privacy is a practice, not a one-time setting.

Small habits can make a meaningful difference. Start with these practical steps.

01 · Review app permissionsAllow only the access an app genuinely needs. Revisit permissions regularly.
02 · Use strong, unique passwordsUse a password manager and avoid reusing the same password across services.
03 · Turn on two-factor authenticationAdd another layer of protection to important accounts wherever available.
04 · Think before sharingCheck who can see a post and whether it reveals identifying details or routines.
05 · Check privacy settingsReview profile visibility, ad preferences, location history and account activity.
06 · Keep devices updatedInstall trusted updates to receive security fixes and improvements.
